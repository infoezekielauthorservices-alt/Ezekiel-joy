A Note on Pricing

Honest, Transparent, and Tailored to Your Project.

Book marketing is not a one-size-fits-all service. Final pricing depends on the book's genre, stage, scope of work, advertising budget, and timeline. I will always confirm costs in writing before any work begins.

No Hidden Fees

Every cost is agreed in advance. You will never be surprised by charges that were not discussed and approved beforehand.

No Sales Pressure

If a package or service is not right for your book, I will say so. The goal is the right fit, not the biggest package.

Flexible Arrangements

Plans can be scaled, combined, or adjusted. Many authors begin with one service and expand once they see how the work develops.
