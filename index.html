A Word from Ezekiel

Strategic Book Marketing, Grounded in Experience.

I am Ezekiel Joy, a professional book marketer who has spent the last eight years helping authors navigate the difficult work of promoting their books. My focus is simple: build thoughtful, honest marketing that places a book in front of the readers most likely to engage with it.

Every campaign I take on begins with the book itself: its genre, its tone, its reader. From there, I shape a strategy that combines creative branding, targeted advertising, reader outreach, and consistent visibility across the platforms that matter.

Why Work With Me

A Steady Hand Through a Noisy Market.

Publishing has never been more crowded. Thoughtful marketing is what separates a book that gets noticed from one that does not.

I.

Strategy Before Spend

Every campaign begins with research: the book, its genre, its reader, and the platforms where that reader already spends time. No budget moves before a strategy exists.

II.

Right Reader, Right Place

Visibility is only valuable when it reaches the right people. I focus on audience precision rather than vanity reach, so marketing budgets go further.

III.

Creative That Earns Attention

From cover design to ad creative, I work on materials that respect the book and speak clearly to the readers most likely to pick it up.

IV.

Clear, Honest Communication

Authors work hard on their books. I keep campaigns transparent, explain decisions in plain language, and share results without inflated claims.

V.

Built for the Long Term

A book's life does not end at launch. I plan for ongoing visibility, backlist marketing, and sustained reader engagement over time.

VI.

Experience Across Genres

Eight years of working with fiction and nonfiction authors across a wide range of genres, from literary debuts to established series.
