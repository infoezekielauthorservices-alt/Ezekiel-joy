A Complete Range of Book Marketing, Thoughtfully Delivered.

From branding and design through advertising, reader outreach, discoverability, and long-term campaign management, these are the services I offer to authors at every stage of their publishing journey.

I. Category One

Book Branding & Creative Design

Before a book can be marketed, it needs a strong visual identity. This category focuses on the design assets and branding materials that shape how readers encounter a book for the first time, from its cover and promotional graphics to the author brand that surrounds it.

Strong branding helps a book look professional, signal its genre clearly, and stand alongside the best titles in its category. It also gives advertising campaigns, social posts, newsletters, and retailer listings a consistent and recognisable look.

Book Promotion & Advertising

Paid advertising is one of the most reliable ways to place a book in front of new readers, provided the targeting, creative, and strategy are handled with care. This category covers the full lifecycle of a book promotion, from launch campaigns to backlist marketing and ongoing optimisation.

Campaigns are planned around the book's genre and audience, with careful attention to budget efficiency, platform performance, and the quality of the creative being tested. The goal is steady, measurable progress rather than short bursts of attention.

IV. Category Four

Amazon & Book Discoverability

Amazon and other retailers are where most readers decide whether to click, read a sample, or buy. This category focuses on the technical and editorial work that improves a book's visibility within search results, categories, and recommendation systems.

Careful keyword research, well-chosen categories, and a clear book description can transform how a book performs, often without any additional advertising spend. These foundations matter for every author, from new releases to long-running backlists.
